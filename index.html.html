Before you use this guide

This is not medical advice

Nothing here diagnoses, treats, or rules out any condition. It explains what a parameter generally measures and what a typical reference range looks like — not what your specific result means for your health.

    Reference ranges shown are typical adult examples for general education. Always compare your results to the range printed on your own report.
